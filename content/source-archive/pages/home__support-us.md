# Support us

## Any amount helps us achieve our goals

In order to align better with our goal of access for all, FaHa has eliminated dues.  We welcome charitable contributions from those that are in a position to give. The organization provides resources for event organizers as well as micro-scholarships for families that express a need.

Check to see if your company is one of the many that offer charitable donation matching.
