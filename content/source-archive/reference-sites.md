# Reference site notes (captured 2026-09-15)

Fetched from the build sandbox. cityyear.org could not be fetched (TLS chain failure from that network); notes for it are from general knowledge and should be re-checked.

## tntp.org
- Utility nav: Events | Careers | Media and News | Contact Us | Donate
- Primary nav: About Us | What We Do (4 service lines, each with a one-line description) | Where We Work | Our Impact | Research and Insights | Tools
- Homepage order: statement hero (h1 is a single mission sentence) → credibility band → "We partner with…" → "What young people need" → Impact → "Our work in action" → "Let's work together" → newsletter
- CTA verbs: Learn More / See How / Explore / Discover. Every section ends in one CTA.

## teachforamerica.org
- Primary nav: Teach In The Corps | Tutor With Ignite | Applicant Resources | How To Apply | Info Sessions | Locations | For Alumni | Where We Work | Donate
- Homepage order: h1 "Make a Difference. Build a Future." → "Choose How to Get Started" (2 paths) → Who We Are → "Your Journey" as a numbered path (find → level up → lead → zoom out → step up) → Where to Begin → Stay Connected
- Pattern worth borrowing: a numbered journey with plain-language steps, and a "choose your path" split near the top.

## cityyear.org (unverified)
- Nav typically: About | Our Impact | Join the Corps | Partner | Donate; homepage leads with a value statement, then stats, then paths for students/schools/donors.

## What carries into the FaHa build
- One mission sentence as the hero heading, no slideshow.
- "Choose your path" split for Resources: Unschooling / Eclectic / Structured (the three approach pages already exist).
- Numbered "How to join" path: Read the rules → Sign up on Zeffy → Join Discord → Find the calendar.
- One CTA per section, verbs not slogans.
- Utility strip in the header for Donate / Membership status.
