## Drivers education

### Getting a License in Virginia

When your child turns 15 years and 6 months old they are now eligible for a learner's permit! [This flowchart](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGEp-1jfNI/88sTuv58XlJoWxgqIPgXUg/view?utm_content=DAGEp-1jfNI&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=editor) helps to step caregivers through the process of obtaining a License in Virginia.

The best practice for the learner's permit is to review the practice test multiple times. Here is the [link to the DMV provided practice test](https://transactions.dmv.virginia.gov/dmv-manuals/#/sections/manual/1).
