What the group does

FaHa is a member organized 501c3 nonprofit group. Members plan events and share them to a community calendar for everyone to attend. The scope of the events can vary but the idea is to be a participating member in the community you want to build.

Faha can do anything you can plan. Our group does park days, hikes, art, Lego club, Science experiments and much more! The board members use the donations to the group to plan some events too, like: potlucks, chess tournaments, dances or festivals. As the resources for the group grow, so can the things the group is able to put together for everyone.

How joining works

  1. Read the rules

    Secular, inclusive, private, community. They are short. Read them on the About page.

  2. Sign up

    Complete the membership form on Zeffy. There are no dues; the form records your agreement to the rules.

  3. Join Discord

    After you submit the form you will see a Discord invite. Events, conversations and the calendar all live there.

  4. Find the calendar

    In Discord, open the "Event Instructions" channel for the calendar link. Then show up.

Already a member? Check your membership status or renew.

Questions

If you have any questions about the group, or you would like to contact the organizers of this group please feel welcome to reach out: helpdesk@fahahome.com. Common questions are answered on the FAQ page.
